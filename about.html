The Story Behind the Stove

From burnt pans to culinary inspiration.

My Journey

I’ve always been obsessed with the way a great meal brings people together, but for a long time, I felt like a bit of a fraud because I couldn’t just "invent" recipes on the fly.

It took a lot of burnt pans and mediocre dinners to realize that inspiration isn't a superpower, it’s a habit. I started documenting my process not because I’m a culinary wizard, but because I know how frustrating that "what’s for dinner?" blank stare can be.

"Inspiration isn't a superpower, it's a habit."
